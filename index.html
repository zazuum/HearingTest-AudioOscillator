Presbycusis is age-related hearing loss, Aging is the most common reason people lose their hearing. Symptoms include having trouble hearing conversations and turning up the volume on the television, cell phones and tablets. Presbycusis is the most common cause of hearing loss, affecting one out of three persons by age 65, and one out of two by age 75.
